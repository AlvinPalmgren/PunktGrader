Good to know

Frequently asked

Do I need an appointment to drop off my bike?
Walk-ins are welcome. If you want a guaranteed turnaround, booking ahead via the form below or a quick phone call locks in your slot.
How long does a service usually take?
A Quick Tune is same-day if dropped off before noon. Full Services turn around in 24 hours, Overhauls in 48. Custom builds depend on parts availability — we'll confirm a date when we spec the build.
Do you service e-bikes?
Yes — most major systems (Bosch, Shimano STEPS, Bafang). We're not a warranty centre for motor internals, but we handle everything around them: drivetrain, brakes, wheels, diagnostics.
Will you work on bikes you didn't sell?
Absolutely. Doesn't matter where you bought it — if it's a bike, we'll fix it.
Do you offer trade-ins or buy second-hand bikes?
We take trade-ins toward new bikes on a case-by-case basis. For straight buyouts, send a few photos through the contact form and we'll come back with an offer.
